Quarterly Product Strategy Report — Q2 2026

Executive Overview
Our product team focused on improving onboarding conversion and reducing
time-to-value for new customers. Three initiatives drove the majority of
impact: guided setup flows, in-app contextual help, and a redesigned pricing
page.

Key Metrics
- Trial-to-paid conversion increased from 12% to 17%.
- Average onboarding completion time dropped from 42 minutes to 28 minutes.
- Support tickets related to billing decreased by 23% after the pricing refresh.

Strategic Priorities for Q3
1. Launch AI-assisted document search for enterprise accounts.
2. Expand integrations with CRM and data warehouse platforms.
3. Invest in performance monitoring for large-file uploads.

Risks and Mitigations
The main risk is slower enterprise procurement cycles. Mitigation includes
pilot programs with shorter contracts and dedicated customer success playbooks.

Conclusion
Q2 results validate the onboarding-first strategy. Q3 should prioritize
search capabilities and enterprise readiness while maintaining conversion gains.
